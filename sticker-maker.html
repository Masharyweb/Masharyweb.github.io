Sticker Maker – a project by masharyweb

I built Sticker Maker (GIF Sticker) because I wanted stickers of my own people and moments, not generic packs. Here is what it does.

Most sticker apps hand you the same packs everyone else already has. This sticker maker works the other way round: you bring the moment, and it becomes a sticker. A clip from last night, your cat doing something ridiculous, a friend's face mid-laugh.

What you can make

  • Animated stickers from video – trim the exact second you want and set the speed from 0.5x to 2x.
  • GIF stickers – search the built-in GIF library or use files from your phone.
  • Still stickers from photos – AI removes the background in one tap.
  • Text and emoji – 11 fonts, 18 colors, layers you can move and resize.

Five steps, about a minute

  1. Tap + and start a new pack.
  2. Pick a video, GIF or photo.
  3. Trim it and adjust the speed.
  4. Add text or emoji, remove the background if you like.
  5. Tap Add and send it in your chats.

No account, no watermark, and editing stays on your phone. Packs hold up to 30 stickers, either all still or all animated.

Yes. Choose any video, keep the part you like, and it becomes an animated sticker.
